Mailrith helps marketing teams and small businesses manage Subscribers and email marketing from Microsoft Copilot Studio. Connect an agent to one Mailrith workspace to review Subscribers and email results, prepare and deliver Broadcasts, and manage Sequences, Automations, targeting, and email Templates.

Account Requirements
You need a Mailrith account, access to a workspace, and an active trial or paid Mailrith subscription for that workspace. New workspace owners must choose a plan and enter credit-card details during checkout before using Mailrith. Invited team members use the workspace owner's subscription and need permission to connect integrations.

Limitations
Each connection works with one Mailrith workspace. Available actions depend on your Mailrith role, the permissions you approve, and the workspace's plan and status. Billing, team administration, account security, and delivery credentials cannot be managed through this connector. Mailrith sends the email, and sending may need the workspace owner to finish Sending Domain setup. Review drafts and run preflight before sending. Sending, scheduling, activation, deletion, and Subscriber delivery-status changes require explicit approval.
